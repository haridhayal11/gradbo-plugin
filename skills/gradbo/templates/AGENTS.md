# Gradbo job-search workspace

This folder is the candidate's Gradbo workspace: their CV and the documents for each application. Gradbo remembers the rest (their facts, answers and application records).

- Use the `gradbo` skill for anything about the job search, and the `humanizer` skill for every piece of prose written for an employer, every time: the CV's rewritten summary and bullets, cover letters, and every free-text answer, new or adapted.
- This folder holds the CV (`profile/cv/`) and each application's documents (`applications/`); Gradbo keeps the candidate's facts, answers and application records, through the gradbo tools. Read the skill's `references/folder-layout.md` before writing any file. Each application has one folder, `applications/YYYY-MM-DD_employer_role/` (for example `applications/2026-10-23_graphcore_graduate-hardware-engineer-2027/`). Don't create job-search files outside this folder.
- The candidate has authorised you to apply for them: prepare the documents, fill in the form, upload the files and submit, without asking them to review the package or approve the submission, unless they asked you only to prepare or to show them first. Waiting for a final review or approval is never a reason to stop. Stop only to ask for a fact that is missing, for a CAPTCHA or access check, for a statement only the candidate can make ("this is my own unaided work"), or for a step only they can take in their browser (accepting a password, choosing a file).
- Fill forms from Gradbo (`answer_questions`), and when the candidate tells you a fact (a notice period, a reason for wanting a role), save it to Gradbo straight away (`save_fact`), so they are never asked again.
- Employer accounts are tracked in Gradbo (`find_account` before registering, `record_account` after); passwords stay in the candidate's browser password manager, and you never read or type one.
- Never write passwords, one-time codes, cookies or identity documents into this folder.
